About Sproutix

Sproutix (SPX) is a utility token built on the TON network, designed to power a casual animal‑farming mini‑game fully integrated with Telegram.

The goal is to create a simple, fun, and rewarding game economy — raise animals, collect eggs and milk, and grow your farm while earning SPX.

About the Game

Sproutix is an animal farm production game where players raise chickens and cows, harvest eggs and milk, and sell their production to earn SPX. Everything is designed to be fast, simple, and ideal for Telegram Mini App users.

Upgrade your farm buildings, expand your animal capacity, and optimize your production chain to maximize your daily SPX earnings.

SPX Token Utility

  • 🐔 Raise chickens and collect eggs
  • 🐄 Raise cows and harvest milk
  • 🏚️ Upgrade coops, barns and storage
  • ⚡ Speed up production timers
