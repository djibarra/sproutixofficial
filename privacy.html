Privacy Policy

Sproutix (SPX) — Last updated: October 2026

Welcome to Sproutix. We are committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect information when you use our Telegram Mini App and website.

1. Information We Collect

When you use the Sproutix Mini App through Telegram, we may receive the following information provided by Telegram:

  • Your Telegram user ID and username.
  • Your first and last name (as set in Telegram).
  • Your language preference.

We do not collect passwords, payment information, or sensitive personal data.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Create and manage your in-game account.
  • Track your farm progress, production, and SPX balance.
  • Send you relevant game notifications through Telegram.
  • Improve the game experience and fix bugs.

3. Game Data

All in-game data (farm buildings, animal levels, egg and milk production, SPX balance) is stored securely in our database and linked to your Telegram user ID. This data is necessary for the game to function correctly.

4. Third-Party Services

We may use third-party analytics or advertising services on our website. These services may use cookies or similar technologies to collect anonymized usage data. We currently use PopCash as our advertising provider. Please review PopCash's privacy policy for more information.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with third parties, except as required by law or as necessary to operate our services (e.g., cloud hosting providers).

6. Data Retention

We retain your game data for as long as your account is active. If you wish to delete your account and all associated data, please contact us via our Telegram channel.

7. Cookies

Our website may use cookies for advertising purposes through our ad provider. You can disable cookies in your browser settings at any time.

8. Children's Privacy

Sproutix is not directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us.

9. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the right to access, correct, or delete the personal data we hold about you. To exercise these rights, contact us through our official Telegram channel.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify users of significant changes via our Telegram community channel. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes your acceptance of the new policy.

11. Contact

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, you can reach us at:
