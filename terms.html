2 Description of Service

SPX Farm is a Telegram-based farming game where users manage a virtual farm, raise animals, grow crops, and earn SPX tokens on the TON blockchain. The service is provided through Telegram's Mini App (WebApp) platform.

  • Manage virtual farms — chickens, cows, and crop fields
